## 2. Unicode Consortium Ethiopic Block Specifications

- **Unicode Standard Version:** 15.0 / 16.0
- **Blocks:**
  - Ethiopic (`U+1200..U+137F`)
  - Ethiopic Supplement (`U+1380..U+139F`)
  - Ethiopic Extended (`U+2D80..U+2DDF`)
  - Ethiopic Extended-A (`U+AB00..U+AB2F`)
- **Reference:** https://unicode.org/charts/PDF/U1200.pdf
